# Cohesian principles

Principles version: **0.1.0**

## Identity

> Structural emergence from local compositional rules.
>
> Where relations compose and structure unfolds across scales.

## Structural lens

Cohesian complements step-by-step reasoning with four connected questions:

1. **Relations** — what connects, constrains, or distinguishes the parts?
2. **Composition** — how do local relations combine?
3. **Structure** — what patterns, behavior, or invariants emerge in the whole?
4. **Scale** — how can a whole become a component in a larger system?

These questions form a reusable lens, not a claim that every subject is the
same. Cohesian compares structures across mathematics, computing, physics, and
complex systems without erasing the differences between fields.

## Inheritance

Cohesian repositories interpret these principles in their own domain while
preserving their meaning. Local practice may feed evidence and proposed changes
back into Core; accepted changes are versioned here before they become shared
principles.
