# Cohesian

## Introduction

Cohesian is an independent educational and research organization founded by
Lucas Masiero in March 2026.

It creates visual essays, written resources, and open-source tools for exploring
how relations, composition, invariants, and transformations produce structure
across mathematics, computer science, programming, physics, and complex
systems.

Cohesian complements step-by-step reasoning with structural and relational
thinking. Its work is guided by four connected ideas:

1. **Relations** — what connects, constrains, or distinguishes the parts?
2. **Composition** — how do local relations combine?
3. **Structure** — what patterns, behavior, or invariants emerge in the whole?
4. **Scale** — how can a whole become a component in a larger system?

This is not a claim that every subject is the same. It is a method for comparing
structures without erasing the differences between fields.

## What Cohesian makes

- **Foundations** — the knowledge repository: a connected graph of papers,
  notes, notebooks, and source material.
- **Studio** — images, videos, and visual explanations developed from Cohesian's
  identity and source knowledge.
- **Loci tooling** — open-source animation tools built on Manim for producing
  educational animations and narrated scenes.
- **cohesian.org** — the public projection of Cohesian's knowledge and media.

## Identity

> Structural emergence from local compositional rules.
>
> Where relations compose and structure unfolds across scales.

Cohesian is for curious learners, builders, and researchers who want to move
between concrete examples and the structures connecting them.
